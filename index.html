The Address

Parking

This is downtown San Diego, so street parking is metered and can fill up fast. Give yourself extra time — we recommend arriving by 1:00 PM for our 1:30 PM appointment.

There's a public parking structure on-site at the County Administration Center, plus several paid lots and garages nearby along Pacific Hwy and Front St. if it's full. exact lot details coming soon

Like any courthouse building, there's a security check at the entrance — plan for a few extra minutes to get through, and avoid bringing anything that wouldn't clear an airport-style screening.
